**Live URL:** https://jgpa2005.github.io/

**Source:** https://github.com/jgpa2005/jgpa2005.github.io

## Move your data (one time)

1. Open your old temporary Slip link
2. Tap **Export backup**
3. Open https://jgpa2005.github.io/
4. Tap **Import backup**
5. Safari → Share → **Add to Home Screen**
6. Delete the old temporary Home Screen icon

Keep using https://jgpa2005.github.io/ forever so updates and transactions stay together.
