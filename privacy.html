Privacy Policy for Slip

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Slip (“the app”) is a simple personal expense tracker.

What data Slip stores

When you add an entry (amount, establishment/place name, type/category, and date), Slip saves it on your device only.

This includes spending types (such as Restaurant or Groceries) and income types (such as Salary).

What Slip does not do

  • Slip does not create an account for your expenses
  • Slip does not upload your expense data to our servers
  • Slip does not sell your data
  • Slip does not show third-party advertising in the app
  • Slip does not track you across other apps or websites for advertising

How your data is used

Your entries are used only to show your history and consolidation totals (week, month, or custom range) inside the app on your device.

Deleting your data

You can remove individual entries in the app. Uninstalling Slip removes the locally stored data from that device (as managed by your device/OS).

Children

Slip is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the “Last updated” date above.

Contact

If you have privacy questions about Slip, contact the developer through the App Store listing support URL / email you provide in App Store Connect.
